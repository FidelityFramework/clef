Inverse existence fixes the family: axes with inverses unify with cancellation, axes without inverses can only be propagated by ordering, and unification modulo a semilattice is not unitary. Fixing fact: `grade-axis/01 §2 closing paragraph line 39`, "Read together, the four facts fix the shape of the annotation before any design preference enters. What looked like one axis is three because the algebra carries three different structures, and the correction records what the algebra already settled." Family membership: `grade-axis/02 §7.1 line 87`, "support is propagated, never unified; parity and dimension are unified, never propagated; principality means most general unifier on the group axes and least fixed point on the lattice axes, each in its native sense."

## The one-sentence answer to the owner's question

There is no choice on C2, C5, the rule of C4, and the blade half of C9: the algebra of each axis, Nelson-Oppen, Matiyasevich and the quantifier-free fragment fix them, and D1 to D5 already stand on the right side of every one. On C1, C3, C6, C7 and C8 the structure, the lifetime and the family are fixed and three decisions remain: the word for the range slot, which the spec settles as a coeffect field on the numeric node outside the unifier; whether a coefficient's range is a marginal box or a projection of a joint manifold, which the spec settles for now as the box with the joint refinement open; and the seal syntax. The amendments the plan needs are wording: erase at emission not never, access ordered with an explicit narrowing node, region and access as enum sorts not measures, the seal as a checked claim not an identity component, and Smith normal form when parity joins. The plan proceeds as written with those five edits.
